## Learnings
- **2026-09-21 — Fixing a wrong identifier does not prove a source is reachable.** Blossom had two independent causes of silence; yesterday's handle repair cleared only the first. A repaired identifier earns one verification walk, not restored status — and an Instagram-only repair can never unlock event content, because IG posts are not indexed at that depth. Appended to `playbook.md`.
- **2026-09-21 — A source that names editions by guest instead of by date can never close a date-watch.** Resonanz, 9 of 9 recent logs, retired. Before scheduling any D-N watch, confirm the source has ever published a dated identifier for that event. Appended to `playbook.md`.
- **2026-09-21 — Read our own note before buying a fact we already own.** The Folk Festival search re-derived a date sitting in `sources.json` since June. This is the exact inverse of yesterday's Blossom error — one trusted a stale record without checking, the other checked without reading the record — and both spend the same budget. Appended to `playbook.md`.
- **Pattern across three runs: the arithmetic and the file-audit keep outperforming the index.** Sep 18 closed three watches by weekday, Sep 19 two by moon-date, Sep 20 found a nulled source in our own file, and today closed two watches and retired a third with one search between them, while eleven searches against the live index produced nothing dated. Under a fixed budget the cheap checks are not a fallback; they are the main instrument.
