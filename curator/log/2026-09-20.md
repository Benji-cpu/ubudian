## Learnings
- **2026-09-20 — Three consecutive empty runs should trigger an audit of our record, not another search.** See the Blossom finding above; generalised into `playbook.md`. The check is free and it audits the beat instead of re-reading the same index, which is the only kind of work that pays under a fixed search budget.
- **2026-09-20 — An undocumented recurring anchor is a recurring tax.** Dragonfly and Mudra both cost a search, produced a reject, and taught prior runs nothing because the anchor was never written down. Recording the anchor makes the *ingestable* case legible: a dated per-edition slug or an explicitly branded special edition.
- **2026-09-20 — The weekday/moon kill has a blind spot: date ranges.** Every cheap kill this month worked on a single claimed date with a claimed weekday. An exhibition run ("Oct 19 – Nov 15") asserts no weekday, so arithmetic cannot touch it and there is no free path to a year. Range-dated discovery events therefore need the one neutral quoted-year query that single-date events can often avoid — budget for it rather than expecting arithmetic to close them.
