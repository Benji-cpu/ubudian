# Ubud restaurants and bars with recurring deals: first 30 (Oct 2026)

Compiled 5 Oct 2026 by web research only (search results and public pages; Instagram bios where they loaded). Nobody was contacted, no forms submitted, nothing signed in. I started from Blue Door, No Más, Indus and Finn's Beach Club's happy-hour guide, verified each against the venue's own page where one loaded, then widened with searches and 2026 guides (Bali Buddies, Bali Holiday Secrets, Honeycombers, Pinstripe's blog). About 50 candidates were checked and 30 kept. Confidence: **high** = venue's own page, readable now; **medium** = third-party guide 2025-26 or venue page that omits the deal; **low** = undated, review-based or old. Result: 11 high, 10 medium, 9 low. Prices are as published (usually ++ for tax and service); where sources disagree the conflict is noted in the JSON and the price left blank if unresolvable. Priority 1 = clear recurring special, own source, central Ubud, fits a visitor crowd; 3 = luxury brunch, unverified, or outside the core areas.

## Notes worth knowing

- laughingbuddhabar.com returned an unrelated gambling page when fetched, so Laughing Buddha is sourced from guides only. Worth a look before anyone links to it.
- No Más and Folk Pool & Gardens show the same WhatsApp (+6281337572686) and phone (+623619080800): likely one operator, so one approach covers both.
- Blue Door's ladies' night is 180k on its own page but 150k in Bali Buddies (Apr 2026); search snippets of the specials page also showed older, lower prices (110k/165k/250k) than the live fetch (135k/185k/265k). Confirm before quoting.
- Cantina's own page names only Sundown Sundays (Sun 17-18); guides claim 2-for-1 daily 16-18.
- Pinstripe (Viceroy, Petulu), Mandapa, Uma Cucina and Indus sit just outside the strict neighbourhood list (Petulu, Kedewatan, Sanggingan); kept because they are Ubud-district and the deals are clear.
- The Sunday brunches (Mandapa, Apéritif, Sokasi, Uma Cucina) are premium, medium confidence, sourced from one April 2026 roundup.
- No Penestanan, Nyuh Kuning, Peliatan or Mas venue with a verifiable recurring deal turned up; Pengosekan has Kakiang (low).
