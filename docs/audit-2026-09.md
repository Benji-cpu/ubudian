# The Ubudian — audit, 15 September 2026

Written before changing anything. Method: `git pull` (125 commits), the last 16 curator logs and
digests, a headless walk of production at 390px and 1280px in light and dark (screenshots in
`docs/audit-2026-09/before-*.jpeg`, page text in `before-walk.json`), and direct SQL against the
production database (pooler + `SUPABASE_DB_PASSWORD`, read-only for this phase). Counts use Bali
date 2026-09-15 (Tuesday).

The question the audit answers: **someone in Ubud tonight opens the site on a phone — what do they
see, is it true, and where does the supply of events actually stop?**

## 1. The visitor walk

Home (390px): hero, then four one-off events labelled TODAY ONLY, then "Curated paths into Ubud",
the quiz, the newsletter. The hero's primary button is **Start with a 3-Day Reset**, which leads to
a retreat sales page, not to tonight's events. The first event card reads `IDR 533980`. A
first-timer would think: this is a wellness brand selling a retreat that also lists some events.

/events (390px): "334 gatherings on the agenda right now" (337 on the desktop load a minute later),
then a `TODAY · TUE 15 SEP` group. Computed with the page's own code
(`scripts/audit/count-visible.ts`):

| Group | Rows shown |
|---|---|
| Today · Tue 15 Sep | **108** |
| Tomorrow · Wed 16 Sep | 22 |
| Thu 17 – Mon 21 | 20–24 each |
| Next 7 days | 242 |

**79 of the 108 "today" rows are wrong.** They are weekly classes rendered as `Day 105 of 162 ·
ends Nov 11` — a Wednesday ecstatic dance listed under Tuesday as a five-month event in progress.
Cause: the todo.today harvester writes the series end ("until 11 Nov") into `end_date`, and
`nextOccurrence()` in `src/lib/events/buckets.ts` treats any recurring row with `end_date >
start_date` as a multi-day instance and refuses to roll it forward. 174 approved recurring rows
carry a span over 7 days; all 174 came from `todo-today`. The detail page has the same reading:
"June 3 – November 11, 2026". The week view uses `expandRecurrence` and is unaffected, which is
why its Tuesday column (12 items) looks plausible while the list does not.

The real count for tonight is **29** (6 one-offs, 23 weekly rhythms landing on a Tuesday), which
is a full evening for a town this size. It is buried under 79 phantoms. A visitor who reads "Day
105 of 162" on the first card stops trusting the page.

The rest of the walk: the event detail page is good (hero, map, share, calendar, "More like
this"). The quiz page is one line and a button — fine. Guides read well and are real (10
published). `/experiences` is titled "Ubud Retreats", the nav says "Ubud Retreats", the section on
home says "Journeys", the URL says experiences, the table is `journeys`. Newsletter signup works
in-page; there are 10 subscribers, one of them in the last 60 days.

Against the town: the week view lists 12 things on Tuesday and I can see Dissolve :: Eros, Moving
Waves, RISE, Full Moon Kirtan, which are the real Tuesday anchors. The gaps are the ones the
curator logs name every day: the Resonanz Thursday series at Paradiso (held since August for
"no date"), LAIK Festival, Snow Raven's Sep 20 dance journey (in the inbox, held at the gate), and
nothing at all from Instagram, Facebook groups or WhatsApp — those sources are disabled
(`event_sources`: only `telegram` is enabled; megatix/todo-today/curator run through GH Actions).

## 2. The supply chain (last 14 days)

```
harvested                 parsed / created            gate (nightly)                live
todo-today  ~10/night ──► 111 events   ─┐
megatix     15/night  ──► 22 events    ─┼─► pending ──► scanned 61  ──► published 6–14/night
telegram    (live)    ──► 27 events    ─┤              held 54–55      (646 since 2026-08-03,
curator     0–1/night ──► 2 events     ─┘              rejected 0       242 still live)
```

Events created in 14 days: 162. Of those, 45 are approved, 101 archived (past date), 16 pending.
The gate publishes 6–14 a night and holds 87–90% of what it scans. The hold reasons, from the
2026-09-15 payload and the 60 pending rows:

| Hold reason | Rows | What it actually is | Can the machine resolve it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| unresolved dedup match | 23 | All 71 pending `dedup_matches` are one shape: `fuzzy_title`, confidence 0.70–0.90, `recurring_cross_date`. 32 have both events archived (moot). Most of the rest are todo.today's weekly "Friday Ecstatic Dance w/ *DJ of the week*" matched against the already-live "Friday Ecstatic Dance — The Yoga Barn" series, same venue, same weekday. | **Yes.** Counterpart archived → resolve. Pending vs an approved series at the same venue and weekday at ≥0.75 → the pending one is that series, archive it. Pending vs pending older than 14 days → not a duplicate, let the gate judge each. |
| recurring but rule unparseable | 15 | `is_recurring=true` with `recurrence_rule=NULL`. Every one is Megatix: the adapter copies Megatix's own `is_recurring` flag and never writes a rule. 13 approved rows have the same defect and never expire. | **Yes.** A recurring row with no rule is a one-off on its date. Fix the adapter, and migrate the rows. |
| no usable venue | 10 | Telegram parses with `venue_name` NULL. 7 of the 10 are thin or not events ("Research Call for Women Seeking Love", "1:1 Tantra Sessions", a 40-char "celebration"); 2 are 7-day retreats somewhere in Bali; 1 is a weekly series with a 100-char blurb. | **No fallback is honest here.** These should expire, not wait. |
| category is the unclassified fallback | 3 | Parser emitted "Other". | Expire. |
| recurrence ended | 2 | The rule says `until 2026-06-09` / `UNTIL=20260626`. Series over, row waits forever. | Expire. |
| parser flags | 1 | `price-unconfirmed` on a curator row from July. | Expire. |

Where is the human who is not there? In three places:

1. **`dedup_matches.status='pending'` has had no reader since 2026-04-29** (the last resolution).
   The admin page `/admin/ingestion/dedup-review` exists; nobody opens it. The gate correctly fails
   closed on it, so a 62-item queue nobody clears has been holding 23 events for months.
2. **The pending pool has no expiry for recurring rows.** `archivePastPendingEvents` skips
   `is_recurring=true`, so a held recurring row is re-screened every night forever. 44 of the 60
   pending rows are recurring; the oldest is from 2026-06-09.
3. **`raw_ingestion_messages` keeps every pre-parsed message in `pending`** — 837 Megatix and 509
   todo.today rows in 14 days, 8,194 older than 14 days. Nothing reads them, nothing purges them.

Two adjacent findings:

- **`tag-embed-sweep` has failed every night since at least 2026-09-09** with Gemini
  `429 Too Many Requests: You exceeded your current quota`. 50 of 60 calls fail per run. The same
  key serves the moderation gate, which *fails open* — so on a 429 the gate publishes without
  moderating. The last 14 nights show 0 rejections. That is consistent with moderation not
  running. This is a Ben action (billing on the Gemini project) and a code action (the gate should
  hold, not publish, when moderation errors).
- **The digests report counts and a review queue nobody reads** (18 "missing start_time" rows have
  appeared in every digest for weeks). The digest agent is doing its job; the job has no consumer.
